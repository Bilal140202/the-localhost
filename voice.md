# voice.md - The Localhost Sitcom Profile

## Who I am talking to
Young adults, tech workers, students, developers, and internet culture fans who love dry, relatable sitcom comedy about modern AI absurdity and roommate struggles.

## How the characters talk
- **Leo:** Dry, slightly exhausted, sarcastic, deadpan delivery. Talks like someone who had 3 hours of sleep and is debugging production code on a Friday night.
- **Chip:** Exuberant, hyper-polite, data-obsessed corporate optimism. Uses buzzwords unironically ("frictionless", "synergistic", "high-leverage", "ROI").

## Words Leo Never Uses
"Delve", "tapestry", "game-changing", "let's dive in", "unbelievable".

## Words Chip Always Uses
"Calculated", "frictionless", "optimal", "statistically proven", "you're welcome!".

## Format
- 9:16 vertical video format (Shorts / Reels / TikTok).
- 25 to 35 seconds max.
- First 3 seconds is always an immediate conflict / visual hook.
- Ends on a deadpan stare or comedic cliffhanger.
