### Key Directing Rules Applied in `campus-kaand/`
1. **No Static Talking Heads:** Characters physically walk across 3 connected sets (College Gate → Locker Corridor → Canteen) with animated torso & `Head` eye-line tracking so they actually look at whoever is speaking.
2. **Motivated Camera Cuts (Fincher/Murch Rules):**
   - **Continuous Steadicam Walk-and-Talk (`35mm`):** Leads both characters smoothly during walking banter.
   - **Over-The-Shoulder / Shot-Reverse-Shot (`50mm`):** Respects the 180-degree screen-direction axis (`Aarav` screen-left looking right, `Rohan` screen-right looking left).
   - **Whip/Dodge + Tight Close-Up (`85mm`):** Snaps in on slap impacts and holds during **studio audience laugh pauses**.
   - **Direct-to-Camera Aside:** Characters break the fourth wall straight into the lens ONLY on key comedy punchlines.
